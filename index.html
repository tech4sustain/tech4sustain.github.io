Conference Objectives

  • Regional Language Dissemination

    To encourage formal dissemination of original research findings and industry best practices in regional languages, primarily Gujarati, with bilingual support in English.

  • Scalable Solutions

    To nurture practical, scalable and impactful solutions in areas such as renewable energy, green technologies, artificial intelligence, data science, smart cities, Industry 4.0/5.0, semiconductor systems, climate resilience, smart mobility, sustainable infrastructure, and disaster management.

  • Research & Innovation

    To promote research and innovation in emerging technological domains contributing to sustainable development.

  • National Alignment

    Align institutional research initiatives with national priorities, skilling ecosystems, and innovation frameworks.

  • Inclusiveness & Access

    To strengthen inclusiveness and accessibility in technical education in alignment with AICTE VAANI.

  • Industry – Academia Interaction

    To facilitate strong industry–academia interaction for addressing contemporary technological challenges.

Manuscript Structure

Submission Guidelines
01 Title

Concise, informative and research-specific.

02 Author Details

Name, designation, department, institution and corresponding email.

03 Abstract

Recommended length: 200–300 words.

04 Keywords

Relevant technical and indexing terms.

05 Introduction

Context, motivation, significance and scope.

06 Literature Review

Prior work, limitations and research context.

07 Research Gap

Clearly identified unresolved problem.

08 Objectives

Specific and measurable wherever applicable.

09 Methodology

Dataset, design, tools, models, experiments and metrics.

10 Results

Findings, analysis, tables, figures and validation.

11 Discussion

Interpretation, comparison, implications and limitations.

12 Conclusion

Contribution, outcomes and key findings.

13 Future Scope

Extensions, recommendations and potential impact.

14 Acknowledgement

Funding, laboratories and technical support where applicable.

15 References

Complete references in APA style.
